    Common questions about website costs in Kenya

    How much does it cost to build a website in Kenya?

    It depends on the type of website and what it needs to do. A landing page can start from around KES 5,000, a portfolio from around KES 8,000, a small business site from around KES 15,000, and larger sites such as e-commerce stores or custom web applications from KES 25,000 upward. Use the calculator above for an estimate based on your specific requirements.

    How much does a portfolio website cost?

    A personal portfolio website typically starts from around KES 8,000 to KES 18,000 depending on the number of pages and whether the design is template-based or fully custom.

    How much does a landing page cost?

    A single landing page built around one product, service or event usually starts from around KES 5,000 to KES 12,000.

    How much does an e-commerce website cost?

    An e-commerce website in Kenya typically starts from around KES 25,000 and can exceed KES 80,000 depending on the product catalogue, payment integration and custom features.

    How much does a school website cost?

    A school or institution website generally starts from around KES 25,000 to KES 50,000, covering admissions, programmes, staff profiles and news.

    How much does an NGO or nonprofit website cost?

    An NGO or nonprofit website typically starts from around KES 20,000 to KES 45,000, covering programme pages, impact stories and donation information.

    How much does a restaurant or hotel website cost?

    A restaurant or hotel website generally starts from around KES 15,000 to KES 35,000, covering a menu, gallery, location and booking information.

    How much does a real estate website cost?

    A real estate website with property listings and search functionality typically starts from around KES 25,000 to KES 55,000.

    How much does a church website cost?

    A church or religious organization website generally starts from around KES 10,000 to KES 25,000, covering sermons, events and giving information.

    How much does a medical or clinic website cost?

    A clinic or medical practice website typically starts from around KES 20,000 to KES 45,000, covering services, staff profiles and appointment booking.

    What affects the final cost of a website?

    The main factors are the type of website, the number of pages, whether the design is custom or template-based, and functional requirements such as e-commerce checkout, user accounts, an admin dashboard, SMS or email integration, booking systems, or third-party API integrations, all selectable in the calculator above.
