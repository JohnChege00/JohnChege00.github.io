About Me

Web developer, Machakos University student, and someone who's spent time on the IT support and data side of things too.

I'm John Chege, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Machakos University. My main technical focus is web development: I build websites and web applications using Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL and EJS on the back end, and HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the front end.

That focus is supported by practical experience outside of pure development. I've worked in IT support, handling hardware and software issues and general user support. I've worked with networking and infrastructure, using tools like Cisco Packet Tracer, GNS3, VirtualBox and VMware to understand how systems connect and run. I've also worked with data analytics, mainly in Microsoft Excel, cleaning and analysing records and building reports.

I've spent time on web content management and digitisation of records too, which means I've seen the web development process from more than one angle: not just building a site, but keeping its content current and making sure the records and data behind it are accurate.

Right now, I'm continuing my studies at Machakos University while building projects that reflect this range: from a full clearance and graduation management system, to a digital publication platform, to Excel-based financial workbooks for schools.
