    Common questions about website costs in Kenya

    How much does a website cost in Kenya?

    It depends on the type of website and what it needs to do. A simple landing page can start from around KES 8,000, a personal portfolio from around KES 12,000, a small business website from around KES 20,000, and a full e-commerce website from around KES 45,000 upward. Use the calculator above for an estimate based on your specific requirements.

    How much does a portfolio website cost?

    A personal portfolio website, like the one you're on now, typically starts from around KES 12,000 to KES 25,000 depending on the number of pages, whether the design is template-based or fully custom, and any extras such as a blog or project case studies.

    How much does a landing page cost?

    A single landing page built around one product, service or event usually starts from around KES 8,000 to KES 15,000, depending on design complexity and whether it needs a contact form, analytics or SEO setup.

    How much does an e-commerce website cost?

    An e-commerce website in Kenya typically starts from around KES 45,000 and can go well beyond KES 150,000 depending on the size of the product catalogue, payment integration, user accounts and any custom features you need.

    How much does a school website cost?

    A school or institution website generally starts from around KES 35,000 to KES 70,000, covering pages like admissions, programmes, staff profiles and news, and more with features like online applications or a parent portal.

    What affects the final cost of a website?

    The main factors are the type of website, the number of pages, whether the design is custom or template-based, and functional requirements such as e-commerce checkout, user accounts, an admin dashboard, booking systems, multi-language support or third-party integrations, all of which you can select in the calculator above.

    Is this estimate a final quote?

    No. It's a starting estimate based on typical project scope for planning purposes. Every project is different, so the final price is confirmed after a short conversation about your exact requirements, and formalised in a service agreement before work begins.
